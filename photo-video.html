Photo & Video

Combining photography and video creates a "power couple" for real estate marketing, as each medium handles a different part of the buyer's psychology.

The Winning "Hybrid" Strategy

  • Step 1: The "Hook" (Photos): Use high-res, HDR photos as primary listing images.
  • Step 2: The "Flow" (Video Walkthrough): Provide a clear spatial connection.
  • Step 3: The "Lifestyle" (Social Teasers): Repurpose video into short vertical clips.
